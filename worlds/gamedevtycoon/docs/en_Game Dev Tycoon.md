# Game Dev Tycoon

Run a game studio from the garage to a full development house while the multiworld decides what you can make.

## What gets randomized

Genres, platforms, topics, engine research, business features (Target Audience, Marketing, Sequels, Multi-Genre,
Multi-Platform and Expansion Packs), office upgrades, staff licenses, story events, training and cash. Game sizes can be
shuffled too with an option.

For shuffled research, the item unlocks the feature and doing the research sends a check. The game tells you when you've
researched something you can't use yet.

## What counts as a check

Cash and fan milestones, game releases, console releases, research, R&D lab projects, hires, story events and
achievements.

## Consoles

- A Platform item includes its dev licence. If it arrives before the console launches, you get an early dev kit.
- Retire Platform items are traps that take a console off the market early. One for a console that has already retired
  does nothing.
- If a console you own retires, on its normal date or from a trap, before you've released a game on it, your studio
  saves enough of the dev kit for a single game. Once that game is out, the console is gone.
- If a console left the market before you got its item, fans make their own build of it. Releasing a game on the fan
  build sends the check, but the console maker sues it out of existence a week later and your game is pulled.

## Goal

Keep the studio alive until the retirement year. The success level can also require a cash and fan target.
