# Core Keeper

## What does this randomizer do?

Core Keeper Archipelago turns progression, crafting access, resources, and
selected discoveries into a multiworld experience. Completing enabled checks
in Core Keeper sends their placed items to the appropriate Archipelago player,
while items found by other players are delivered directly through the in-game
mod.

The default configuration randomizes the major progression needed to advance
through Core Keeper while keeping optional and high-count sanity groups
disabled. Individual crafting-station licenses, reward pools, optional checks,
sanity groups, skill experience, Death Link, and the victory goal can be
configured independently.

## What is considered a location?

Depending on the selected options, locations can include collecting materials,
opening locked chests, finding food and seeds, defeating enemies and bosses,
reaching skill levels, meeting merchants, hatching pets, catching fish, and
acquiring equipment or valuables. Disabled optional groups do not contribute
locations to the generated room.

## What items can be randomized?

Progression includes crafting-station licenses and other access required by the
chosen goal. Remaining locations are filled from the enabled physical-item,
equipment, skill-point, cache, and legendary reward pools. Received physical
items are delivered to the Core Keeper character by the client mod.

## What is the goal?

The selected goal determines the required boss chain and victory event. The
client reports completion to Archipelago when that in-game goal is completed.
