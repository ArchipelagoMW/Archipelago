# Mega Man X5

## What does randomization do to this game?

The eight Maverick weapons, Heart Tanks, and armor capsule contents are shuffled
into the multiworld item pool. Defeating bosses, collecting Heart Tanks, and
reaching armor capsules are location checks that send items to the multiworld.

All eight Maverick stages are open from the start, as in the vanilla game.

## What is the goal?

Defeat all eight Mavericks (collecting their weapons), then reach and defeat
Sigma.

## What items and locations get shuffled?

Items: the 8 special weapons, 8 Heart Tanks, and 8 armor parts (Falcon and Gaea
sets). Locations: each stage's boss, Heart Tank, and armor capsule, plus the
intro stage completion.

## What does another world's item look like in Mega Man X5?

Checks are detected and items are delivered by the Archipelago client while the
game runs in BizHawk; received items are applied directly to your save state
(weapons become usable, Heart Tanks raise max HP, and so on).

## Status

Early development. The world generates; the game client is in progress.
